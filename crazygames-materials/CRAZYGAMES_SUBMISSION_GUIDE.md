## 四、操作说明文案（Controls — 直接复制粘贴）

**【英文版（直接粘贴至 Controls 字段）】**
```markdown
### Desktop (Keyboard & Mouse)
- **W / Up Arrow**: Throttle / Accelerate (auto-shifts into Drive)
- **S / Down Arrow**: Brake / Hold to Auto-Reverse
- **A, D / Left, Right Arrows**: Steer Left / Right
- **Shift / N**: Activate NOS Nitro Boost
- **Space / H**: Honk Horn (interact with traffic ahead)
- **V / L**: Toggle High-Beams (tap rapidly twice to flash high-beams at cars ahead)
- **F**: Deploy Aero-Jet Flight / Drone Skill
- **J / K**: Superbike Wheelie / Stoppie Stunts
- **Z / X**: 180° Drift U-Turn / 360° Spin Stunt
- **Q / E**: Left / Right Turn Signals (`2` for Hazard Lights)
- **B / 3**: Quick Driver Emote (+30 PTS street interaction)
- **C**: Switch 1st-Person Cockpit / 3rd-Person Chase Camera
- **G**: Open 3D Garage & Custom Paint Workshop
- **R**: Recover / Reset Vehicle
- **Mouse Wheel or [ / ]**: Shift Gears (P / R / N / D)
- **P / Esc**: Pause Game

### Mobile & Tablet (Touch Controls)
- **Bottom-Right Pedals**: Hold Throttle to accelerate, Brake to slow down or reverse, and tap **NOS** for nitro boost.
- **Bottom-Left Steering**: Use the on-screen Left/Right arrows (or switch to the virtual steering wheel in Settings).
- **Top Bar**: Tap **Garage** to switch vehicles/paints, **Camera** to toggle view, or **Guide** to replay the interactive tutorial.
```
